What We'll Do
Four areas of improvement, addressed concurrently to unlock the full potential of Pristine's TikTok presence.
⚙️
Campaign Strategy
Campaign Architecture Rebuild
Restructure the campaign from the ground up to unlock the algorithm's full optimisation potential across a broader and more strategic product set.
  • Expand SKU coverage per campaign to enrich algorithm learning and surface more conversion opportunities
  • Build a structured campaign framework with clear segmentation by product category and intent
  • Optimise budget allocation and campaign settings to maximise conversion efficiency from day one
📡
Audience Growth
Upper Funnel Audience Development
Build a continuous cold funnel that grows the brand's engaged audience base and feeds fresh, high-quality signals into the GMV Max algorithm.
  • Activate TikTok Smart+ campaigns across Reach, Video Views and Follower Growth objectives
  • Systematically expand the brand's engaged audience base on the platform over time
  • Create a self-reinforcing loop: broader audiences → richer algorithm signals → higher sustained ROAS
✂️
Creative Management
Monthly Creative Sniping Cadence
Establish a rigorous monthly creative review cycle to ensure budget is always directed toward the strongest-performing assets in the rotation.
  • Review all active creatives monthly against cost-per-order and ROI performance benchmarks
  • Pause budget on underperformers and scale spend behind top-converting videos
  • Introduce fresh creative in rotation consistently to sustain algorithm performance signals
🏷️
Brand Consistency
Brand & Catalog Alignment
Address visual inconsistencies across the product catalog to ensure a cohesive and trustworthy brand experience as impressions and reach scale up.
  • Align all product imagery and visual assets with the Pristine brand identity across listings
  • Resolve naming and presentation inconsistencies to remove customer recognition friction
  • Build brand familiarity consistently across every customer touchpoint at scale
